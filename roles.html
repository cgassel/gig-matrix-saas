Each role in detail

Administrator

Can
  • See and change every booking and contract, including pay and commission.
  • Create, and approve, all logins: team, venue and band.
  • Assign one or several agents to any band and approve agents' band requests.
  • Name a backup to cover their own bookings when away.
  • Manage billing, and see agency-wide earnings, commission by agent and the team list.
  • Set the company time zone (Admin Dashboard, Company Settings) and restore archived bands.
Cannot
  • See a password. Every password is stored scrambled, even from administrators.

Booking Agent

Can
  • See the whole booking calendar (venue, band, date, time, status) so double-booking is avoided.
  • See pay, commission and contracts for their own bookings, and change or delete them.
  • Add venues and bands. A new band must have at least one agent chosen and goes to an administrator for approval.
  • Request a band portal login for a band they work on, or a venue portal login for any venue. An administrator approves it.
  • Archive a band they work on once it has bookings. It disappears from lists and suggestions, but its history stays. An administrator can restore it.
  • Add a colleague as cover on a band (optionally with an end date) for vacation or time off.
  • Name a backup to cover their bookings.
  • Get notifications (bell) for venue requests on their bands.
Cannot
  • See or change another agent's pay, commission, contracts or bookings, unless named as their backup.
  • Create team logins, approve requests, or assign agents to a band outright.
  • See an administrator's email or role. Administrators appear by name only.
  • See agency-wide earnings or other agents' money.

Backup Agent (covering for someone)

Any booking agent can name another agent as their backup, with an optional end date, and can remove them at any time. Administrators can do the same for themselves.

Can, for the covered person's bookings only
  • See pay and commission, and the contracts.
  • Change booking details if something needs fixing.
Cannot
  • Delete the covered person's bookings.
  • Gain any administrator power. Covering for an administrator gives their bookings and nothing else: no team list, no approvals, no agency-wide money, no admin email.

Access ends automatically on the end date, or the moment the backup is removed.

Venue Portal

Can
  • See its own bookings and schedule.
  • Search for available bands by date and pay range, and see suggestions.
  • Send a booking request. The band's agents are notified.
  • Update the venue's own preferences and weekly schedule.
Cannot
  • See band contact details. Everything goes through the agent.
  • See any other venue's data, or booking money.
  • Confirm a booking by itself. An agent handles it.

Band Portal

Can
  • See its own gigs, calendar, counts and earnings.
  • Mark the dates it is available, with a note.
  • Connect its own Google Calendar.
Cannot
  • See any other band, any venue's private data, or the agency's finances.
  • Create or change bookings.

Setting up your company: first day

  1. Confirm your email and set your password from the link we send when you start your trial.
  2. Add your team. Admin Dashboard → Team Logins → Add Team Login. Choose Booking Agent for staff who book, Administrator only for people who should see everything.
  3. Add venues and bands. For each band, assign the agent or agents responsible. Set its pay ranges for each type of gig (for example, clubs with lights and sound versus clubs with no production).
  4. Give portal logins to venues and bands that should have them. Band and venue logins asked for by an agent wait in Approvals on the Admin Dashboard until you approve.
  5. Ask each agent to name a backup (the 🤝 Backup button) before their first holiday.
  6. Subscribe before the trial ends (Billing). After the trial or if a subscription is canceled, the account becomes read-only. Nothing is deleted.

Common situations

An agent is going on vacation

Two separate things. For their bookings, they name a backup, who can then see and fix those bookings. For their bands, they (or an administrator) add a colleague as cover on each band, with an end date, so venue requests reach the colleague too.

An agent adds a new band

They must pick the agent or agents for it. An administrator gets a notification and approves or rejects, which also approves any portal login requested with it. The agent is notified of the decision.

A venue wants to book a band

The venue searches by date and pay range, picks a band and sends a request. Every active agent on that band is notified and handles the booking. The venue never sees the band's contact details.

An agent leaves the company

An administrator reassigns their bands, and anything that needs their bookings to be seen goes through an administrator, who can see and change everything.

An administrator is away

The administrator names a booking agent as their backup. That agent can handle the administrator's bookings but sees nothing else of the admin side.

Privacy summary

  • Pay and commission are visible only to the booking's agent, that agent's backup and administrators.
  • Only the booking's agent, their backup or an administrator can change a booking. Only the agent or an administrator can delete it.
  • Companies are completely separate. One company can never see another's data.
  • Portal logins (venue and band) can only ever see their own information.
