Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Gig Matrix LLC ("Gig Matrix", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to our website, web app and mobile apps (the "Service"). Our customers are businesses, mainly booking agencies in the United States. Our Terms of Service also apply.

1. Our role

For the information about your account and billing, we decide how it is used, and this policy describes that. For the business information a customer's team enters into the Service (for example band, venue and booking details, and the contact details of people at those venues and bands), the customer decides what is entered and why. We handle that information on the customer's behalf, only to run the Service for them. If you are a venue or band contact and have questions about your information, please contact the agency that manages you. We will help them respond.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, operate and support the Service, including showing the right information to the right role.
  • To create and manage accounts, process your subscription, and send service emails such as sign-up confirmations, contract and booking notices, and billing messages.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent fraud and abuse, enforce our Terms, and meet legal obligations.
  • To fix problems, maintain and improve the Service.
  • With your connection and permission, to add events to your Google Calendar.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share information with

We use trusted service providers to run the Service. They may only use the information to provide their services to us:

  • Supabase — hosting, database and sign-in.
  • Stripe — subscription payments.
  • Resend — sending email.
  • Google — only if you choose to connect a calendar.
  • LocationIQ — turning venue addresses into map locations (an address is sent to them for this).
  • GitHub — hosting our website files.

Inside a company, information is shared with the people the company's administrator has given access to, according to their role. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a sale or merger of our business (in which case we will tell you if your information becomes subject to a different policy). Each company's data is kept separate from other companies' data.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

We use only what is necessary to keep you signed in and make the Service work (such as your sign-in session stored in your browser). We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not currently use analytics or tracking tools. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

6. Google user data

If you connect Google Calendar, we use that access only to add booking events you ask us to create. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect at any time, and you can also remove access in your Google account settings.

7. How long we keep information

  • While an account is active, we keep its information so the Service works.
  • If a subscription ends, the company's account becomes read-only and we keep its data for 90 days so it can be viewed, exported or restored. After that we may permanently delete it. A company can ask for earlier deletion (see "Contact us" below).
  • Unconfirmed sign-up requests are deleted after a short time. Security and abuse-prevention records are kept for as long as needed for those purposes.
  • We may keep information longer where the law requires, or to resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. Backups are removed on a regular cycle.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), one-way scrambling of passwords, separation between companies' data, and role-based limits on who can see sensitive details such as pay amounts. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your password confidential, and tell us promptly if you think an account has been accessed without permission.

9. Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, and you can ask us to stop sending non-essential emails. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, such as the right to know, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To make a request, email us at the address below. We may need to verify who you are, and for business data entered by an agency we may direct you to that agency. We aim to respond within 30 days.

10. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Where information is processed

We are based in the United States and our customers are in the United States. Our service providers may process information in the United States and in other countries where they operate.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make material changes, we will notify you by email or in the Service before they take effect, and we will update the date at the top.
